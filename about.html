Our Story

Born in Mirissa, Built on Trust

Sea & Safari Tours started with a simple goal: share the magic of Sri Lanka's southern coast with travelers from around the world — safely, responsibly, and with genuine local hospitality.

From early-morning whale watching cruises to hidden river kayaking routes, every experience is led by skilled local guides who know these waters intimately. We combine premium service with fair pricing, so your adventure feels special without the stress.
